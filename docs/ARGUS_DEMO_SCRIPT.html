Segment 1 — Intro + Reproducibility ~1 min

"This is Argus — a governed, explainable AI copilot for banking risk. It detects fraud typologies, surfaces evidence with policy citations, drafts regulatory filings, and enforces role-based data access. Everything you see runs on a single Snowflake account — no external services."
The argus/scripts/ folder: 13 numbered SQL scripts (00 through 12), one semantic view YAML, one agent YAML, and a Streamlit app. Everything is idempotent — run the scripts in order and the full platform deploys from scratch.
"The entire stack deploys from 13 SQL scripts. Dynamic Tables for the data pipeline, Cortex Search for policy RAG, a Semantic View for governed queries, a Cortex Agent for the intelligence layer, and a Streamlit dashboard for the UI. Let me show you what it does."
Snowflake features used: Dynamic Tables (incremental + full refresh), Cortex Search Service, Semantic View, Cortex Analyst, Cortex Agent (6 tools), Masking Policies, 7 RBAC roles, Streamlit-in-Snowflake, SYSTEM$SEND_EMAIL, Tasks (daily digest), Stored Procedures (Python + SQL).

Segment 2 — Detection Pipeline via Dynamic Tables ~3 min

"The data pipeline is entirely Dynamic Tables. Raw banking data — customers, accounts, 100,000 transactions, loans, counterparties, device sessions — flows through seven conformed tables that deduplicate and standardize automatically. At the end of the chain, a signals table evaluates four fraud typologies every 15 minutes."
SHOW DYNAMIC TABLES IN DATABASE ARGUS_RISK_COPILOT;
The 8 Dynamic Tables — 7 CONFORMED (incremental, DOWNSTREAM lag) feeding into 1 GOVERNANCE.SIGNALS_ALERTS (full refresh, 15-minute lag). All ACTIVE.

Segment 3 — Cortex Agent via CoCo CLI ~3 min

SIGNAL → EVIDENCE → FINDING → REPORT

"The Argus agent has six tools — a data analyst backed by the semantic view, a policy search engine over six regulatory documents, case management, finding drafts, audit logging, and notification dispatch. Let me walk through a real investigation."

Step 1 — SIGNAL: Pick the alert

Query the top CRITICAL money mule signal. Show: Customer 318, Account 2511, confidence 0.90.
"Customer 318's account was dormant for 60 days, then received 12 inbound NEFT credits totaling INR 19.2 lakh from 12 unrelated counterparties across seven countries — Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nigeria, Singapore, Germany, and the US. Two RTGS debits of INR 5.96 lakh each followed within 10 hours."

Step 2 — EVIDENCE: Transactions + Policy

Show the 14 evidence transactions — 12 inbound NEFT credits with counterparty names and countries, then 2 outbound RTGS debits highlighted in red.
Search the policy engine: "What are the money mule indicators under AML policy?"
Result: AML Policy Manual, Section 4.3 — "Account dormant 60+ days... 10+ inbound from unrelated counterparties within 48 hours... rapid outbound within 24 hours... CRITICAL severity."
"All three indicators confirmed. The agent cites the exact policy section — Section 4.3. Now let's create a case."

Step 3 — Open Case

CALL GOVERNANCE.UPSERT_CASE(..., 'MONEY_MULE', 318, 'OPEN', 'P1', 'Priya Sharma', 'COMPLIANCE_OFFICER')
Result: CASE-88457432 created, status OPEN, priority P1.

Step 4 — FINDING: Draft the STR

CALL GOVERNANCE.DRAFT_FINDING_REPORT('CASE-88457432', 'STR', ...) with:
• Full narrative referencing all 14 transaction IDs
• Evidence summary: inbound TXN 200216–200227, outbound TXN 200228–200229
• Policy citations: AML Section 4.3, RBI Master Direction Section 3.2, STR Template Section B.2
• Confidence: 0.90
Result: RPT-11358555 created, status DRAFT.
"The draft is created with a full evidence chain and three policy citations. But it's marked DRAFT — nothing goes to FIU-IND without a human approving it. That's the human-in-the-loop principle."

Step 5 — REPORT: Approve + Close

CALL GOVERNANCE.APPROVE_REPORT('RPT-11358555', 'APPROVE')
Result: Status → APPROVED, approved_by → PRIYAMSINGHJHS.
Close the case: UPSERT_CASE(..., 'CLOSED_CONFIRMED')
Result: Resolution → STR_FILED. Notification dispatched.
"Case closed. STR filed. Every step — from signal detection to case closure — is recorded in the audit trail with reviewer name, role, timestamp, and comment."
The 4-row audit trail: STATUS_CHANGE (OPEN → UNDER_REVIEW) → ESCALATION → REPORT_APPROVED → CLOSURE.

Segment 4 — Streamlit Dashboard ~3 min

"Everything we just did in the CLI can also be done through the Streamlit dashboard. It's role-aware — each role sees only the pages and data they're authorized for."

Page 1 — Alert Queue

Open the dashboard. Show the Alert Queue filtered to CRITICAL + HIGH. Metrics at top: 85 Total Alerts, 18 Critical, Open Cases count, Avg Confidence. Sortable table with signal_id, typology, severity, confidence, case status.
"This is the analyst's inbox. Filterable by typology and severity. Every alert links to a case."

Page 2 — Case Detail (the core workflow)

Select a case that has NO findings yet. Show three tabs: Evidence Timeline, Finding, Actions.
"The Evidence Timeline tab shows the raw signal JSON — every transaction ID, counterparty, and geolocation. The Finding tab is empty. Watch what happens when I click 'Ask Argus to Draft Finding'."
Click "Ask Argus to Draft Finding". Spinner: "Argus is gathering evidence..." Then the Review Draft Finding panel appears with:
  • Editable Narrative (pre-filled by the agent with transaction IDs, amounts, policy citations)
  • Editable Policy Citations (auto-retrieved from Cortex Search)
  • Editable Evidence Summary
  • Confidence slider (pre-set from the signal)
  • Report Type auto-defaulted (MONEY_MULE → STR)
"Argus drafted the narrative, retrieved the policy citations, and parsed the evidence — but nothing is saved yet. The reviewer can edit any field. Only when they click 'Submit Finding and Draft Report' does it write to the database."
Click "Submit Finding & Draft Report". Success message. The Finding tab now shows the finding with narrative and policy ref. The Draft Reports table shows the report as DRAFT.

Page 3 — Regulatory Reporting

Navigate to Regulatory Reporting → Draft Filings Queue. Show the draft report. Select it — narrative, evidence, citations displayed. Click "Approve Filing".
"The Compliance Officer reviews the full draft — narrative, evidence chain, policy citations, confidence score — then approves. The status changes to APPROVED, the approver's name and timestamp are recorded."

Page 4 — Ask Argus (Chat)

Type: "Show me the top 5 customers by transaction volume who also have a CRITICAL signal". Argus responds with data from the semantic view + policy context.
"Ask Argus is a chat interface that routes every question through the semantic view and policy search. Quick-action buttons — View Transactions, Draft Finding, Escalate — are one click away."

Page 5 — Liquidity & Credit

Show the LCR Proxy Trend chart, NPA/DPD Buckets bar chart (CURRENT, SMA-0/1/2, SUB-STANDARD, DOUBTFUL), and Early Warning Borrowers table (bureau score drops ≥ 50).
"Credit and liquidity risk are monitored alongside fraud. The LCR ratio is computed from the General Ledger. NPA classification follows RBI's DPD buckets. Early warning flags any borrower with a bureau score drop of 50 or more."

Page 6 — Audit Log

Show the Question Log (searchable) and Reviewer Actions History. Every agent question, every status change, every approval is recorded.
