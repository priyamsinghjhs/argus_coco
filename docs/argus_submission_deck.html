The Problem & Our Solution

The Problem

  • Banks detect fraud reactively, with siloed teams and manual processes
  • Compliance officers lack unified view across fraud, AML, credit risk, and liquidity
  • Investigation evidence is scattered; regulatory filings are error-prone
  • No governed AI layer: LLMs hallucinate without data grounding
  • PII exposure risks during cross-team collaboration

Argus: The Governed AI Copilot

  • Automated detection of 4 fraud typologies via rule-based Dynamic Tables
  • Evidence-first AI: Cortex Agent queries only through Semantic View + Policy Search
  • SIGNAL → EVIDENCE → FINDING → REPORT governance loop with human-in-the-loop approval
  • Column-level PII masking with 7 RBAC roles
  • Full audit trail: every query, action, and notification logged

5 Masking Policies (CURRENT_ROLE)

  • MASK_NAME: customer names
  • MASK_EMAIL: email addresses
  • MASK_PHONE: phone numbers
  • MASK_ADDRESS: physical addresses
  • MASK_DEVICE_ID: device identifiers

Approval Gate

  • APPROVE_REPORT stored procedure (EXECUTE AS OWNER)
  • Validates role, validates status, sets approved_by
  • Logs to REVIEWER_ACTIONS + dispatches notification
  • Only DRAFT/RETURNED reports can be actioned
Verified: FRAUD_ANALYST sees ***MASKED***, ****@domain.com, +91XXXXX1234. COMPLIANCE_OFFICER sees full plaintext.
